With over 15 years of dedicated piano and cello teaching across California (El Dorado Hills, Folsom, Sacramento), Singapore, and Taiwan, Mrs. Cloris Huang balances conservatory precision with compassionate mentorship.

An active member of the Music Teachers' Association of California (MTAC), she prepares students with distinction for Carnegie Hall debuts, Certificate of Merit (CM) evaluations, ABRSM examinations, and chamber collaborations.
